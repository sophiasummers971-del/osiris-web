## Rollback and retirement procedure requiring authorization

For this design PR: close it or revert its documentation commit; production remains untouched.

For a future **authorized** staging experiment: first stop new grants and confirm DB gate FROZEN; quarantine ingress/egress, stop workers and independently confirm all process trees and outstanding DB sessions/queries are gone. Missing confirmation leaves STOP_UNVERIFIED/UNKNOWN and blocks reuse. Preserve authority/DB/checkpoint hashes, burned manifests, all pending intents and evidence **before** changing artifacts.

A code rollback never re-enables an unfenced artifact, rewrites an approved plan or clears consumed IDs. New code/target requires fresh version/approval. A database restore follows the new-endpoint/incarnation reconciliation protocol; no in-place availability reset or automatic retries.

After owner/reconciler accept final outcomes and export verification, a named staging custodian may separately authorize credential revocation, scoped worker/namespace/DB/host teardown and billing shutdown. Retain independent manifests/tombstones under approved policy; do not delete a namespace/bucket to erase replay evidence. Verify teardown and charges with read-only metadata, preserve unresolved cases and record who authorized each destructive step. No future cleanup in this paragraph is authorized now.
