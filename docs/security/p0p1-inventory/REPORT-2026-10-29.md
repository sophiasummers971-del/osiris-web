PostgreSQL's [row-security documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html) establishes that BYPASSRLS/owners bypass row policies and that TRUNCATE/REFERENCES are outside row security. [GRANT documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-grant.html) distinguishes schema and function privileges. These semantics do not prove a reachable public exploit.
